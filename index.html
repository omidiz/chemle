      How to play

      Name today’s mystery molecule in 6 guesses.

      • Drag the 3D model to rotate it, pinch or scroll to zoom.
      • Each wrong guess is compared with the answer:
      • Rings✓2Green with a check: same as the answer.
      • Weight≈180.2↑Yellow with an approximately sign: close; the arrow says the answer is higher.
      • Atoms✗9↓Grey with a cross: far off; the arrow says the answer is lower.

      ✓ exact≈ close✗ off↑↓ answer is higher / lower

      • Weight, atom count, rings, type and shared functional groups are compared.
      • A hint appears after your 3rd and 5th wrong guess.
      • Everyone gets the same molecule. A new one arrives at midnight.
